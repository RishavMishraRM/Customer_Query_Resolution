# SOP Identifier: SOP-ATM-004
# Policy Title: Standard Operating Procedure for Failed ATM Cash Dispensation

## 1. Overview
Covers cases where a customer attempted cash withdrawal at an On-Us (own bank) or Off-Us (other bank) ATM, their account was debited, but either partial cash or no cash was dispensed by the machine.

## 2. Bank SLA & Reversal Timeline
- Automated Auto-Reversal SLA: As mandated under banking guidelines for ATM transactions, auto-reversal of debited amount takes place within **T+5 calendar days**.
- If credit is not received within T+5 calendar days, the customer is eligible for delay compensation of **₹100 per day** for delays attributable to bank settlement.

## 3. Agent Protocol
1. Gather details: ATM Location/Terminal ID, Date, Approximate Time, and Amount requested.
2. Confirm if the customer has the physical ATM transaction slip.
3. Check the switch electronic journal (EJ) log if available.
4. If transaction is within T+5 days: Provide the reassurance script and register a tracking SR number.
5. If transaction is past T+5 days: Escalate immediately.

## 4. Mandatory Escalation
- Escalate to: **ATM Reconciliation Cell (Queue: ATM-OPS-SLA)**.
- If delay exceeds 7 days, trigger Senior Operations Manager review.

SOP Identifier: SOP-CARD-003
Policy Title: Standard Operating Procedure for Disputed Credit and Debit Card Charges

1. PURPOSE & APPLICABILITY:
This procedure applies to cardholders reporting duplicate charges, billing errors, unrecognized merchant transactions, or failed POS swipes resulting in posted debits on domestic and international payment cards.

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS BY CALL CENTER AGENT:
Step A: Verify Cardholder Identity using permitted non-sensitive questions (Date of Birth, Mother's maiden name, registered email domain). NEVER request or collect CVV, ATM PIN, or full 16-digit card number. Only verify the last 4 digits displayed on screen.
Step B: Check Transaction Ledger. Confirm if the disputed transaction is in 'PENDING/HOLD' or 'SETTLED/POSTED' state.
- If in 'HOLD' state: Inform customer that authorizations usually release automatically within 3 to 5 business days if uncollected by merchant.
- If in 'SETTLED' state: A formal Chargeback dispute must be initiated.

3. DISPUTE TYPES & TIMELINES:
- Category 1: Duplicate Processing (Customer swiped once, billed twice by same merchant for identical amount).
  Turnaround Time: Temporary shadow credit provided within 7 business days; investigation concluded within 14 business days.
- Category 2: Fraudulent / Unrecognized Online Transaction.
  Immediate Action: Block card immediately, reissue replacement card, and transfer call to Fraud Risk Management (FRM) team.
- Category 3: Merchandise Not Received or Defective Goods.
  Customer must first submit merchant invoice and proof of merchant cancellation attempt.

4. ESCALATION & CHARGEBACK FILING:
If duplicate swipe amount exceeds ₹5,000, agent must log Chargeback Form CB-01 with Acquiring Switch within 60 days of statement date.
Escalate to: Tier-2 Card Disputes Desk (Queue: CRD-DISPUTE-L2).
Agent Warning: Never offer guaranteed compensation or statement write-offs without approval from the dispute settlement manager.
